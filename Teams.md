#### Club History:

Housed within the stone walls of a historic medieval fortress, the pitch is bordered by ancient battlements, and royal banners hang from the spectators' galleries. Under the current era, they compete aggressively to claim territorial pride. Their colors, hsl(153, 75%, 35%) and hsl(213, 90%, 65%), light up the holographic columns before every match, driven by a highly passionate local assembly.
